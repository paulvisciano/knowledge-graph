Here are some excellent beginner-friendly trails near Yosemite:

Lower Yosemite Falls — A flat 1-mile loop with stunning waterfall views. Accessible year-round.

Mirror Lake — 2.4 miles round-trip, mostly paved. Beautiful reflections of Half Dome in spring.

Bridalveil Fall — Short 0.5-mile walk to the base of the 617-foot waterfall.

Assistant

Mirror Lake is the photographer's choice. In spring and early summer, the still water creates perfect reflections of Half Dome. Go early morning for the best light and fewest crowds.
